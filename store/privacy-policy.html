Whatnow Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Overview

Whatnow ("we", "our", "the app") is a task management application designed to help users manage tasks and reduce decision fatigue. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

Account information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and display name via Supabase Authentication (which supports Google Sign-In and email/password).

Task data: Tasks you create, including names, descriptions, types, time estimates, and completion statistics. This data is stored securely in our Supabase database.

Usage data: Points earned, tasks completed, time spent, and rank progression to power the gamification features.

Advertising identifiers: With your consent, we may collect device advertising identifiers to serve personalised ads. See the Advertising section below.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance: Account data and task data are necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest: Usage analytics to improve the app experience.
  • Consent: Personalised advertising and tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time through the "Manage Privacy" option in your profile settings or by contacting us.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality
  • To sync your tasks across devices
  • To calculate leaderboard rankings within groups you join
  • To process premium subscriptions via in-app purchases
  • To serve ads to free-tier users (with your consent where required)

Advertising

Free-tier users may see ads served by Google AdMob. In the EEA and UK, we request your consent before serving personalised ads via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). If you decline consent, you will see non-personalised ads or no ads.

On iOS, we also request permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before collecting your device's advertising identifier.

You can change your ad preferences at any time through the "Manage Privacy" option in your profile settings.

Third-Party Services

Supabase: Backend database and authentication. Supabase Privacy Policy

Google AdMob: We display ads to free-tier users. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalisation (with your consent). Google Privacy Policy

RevenueCat: Subscription management for premium purchases. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and entitlement data. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Retention

Your data is stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure. Task data may also be cached locally on your device for offline access using an encrypted local database.

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the app's functionality.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Restrict processing: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent: Change your ad/tracking preferences at any time via "Manage Privacy" in profile settings
  • Lodge a complaint: Contact your local data protection authority (UK: ICO at ico.org.uk)

International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Children's Privacy

Whatnow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

Contact
